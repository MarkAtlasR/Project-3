Event Location

Tau Ceti star system at The Hail Mary Spacecraft orbiting Tau Ceti E

Schedule

4:00pm - Arivel

Guests will arrive at the Hail Mary spacecraft in orbit. Make sure you park your vessel at a higher orbit to prevent collision, that would be very bad.

4:30pm - Down Time

The party officially starts! Feel free to explore the ship in zero-G, meet new people and Eridians. Enjoy the beautiful green and red atmosphere of Adrian (Tau Ceti E) from the many windows throughout the ship.

5:30pm - Gravity Dance

At this time the ship's centrifuge (artificial gravity) will be active so try not to be in the air at that time… We will also bring out food and drinks and start the dance competition!

6:00pm - Birthday Wishes

We will take a moment to give a toast to Rocky and honor his 292 birthday, yes that is normal for Eridians. We will also be rolling out desserts like Tiramisu and Ice Cream.

6:30pm - Game Time

Nearing the end but that doesn't mean the fun will die down. We will have a community game event with prizes for winners including one big prize for the lucky winner of the raphael

7:30pm - Departure

Late into the day we will start wrapping up. Collect your personal items plus your guest bag for coming to visit and give one last fist bump for Rocky before leaving. We appreciate you coming to visit and have a safe trip.
